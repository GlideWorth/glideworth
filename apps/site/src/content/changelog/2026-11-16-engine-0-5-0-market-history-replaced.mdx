---
title: "Engine 0.5.0: market history replaced"
date: 2026-11-16
version: Engine 0.5.0
tags: [engine, fix]
---

- **Market history with open licences.** The UK series from 1871 to 2020 (non-commercial terms) and the bundled US
  series from 1871 (no licence) were removed. The defaults are now US since 1928 and UK since 1983.
- **Plans that named a removed set** run on UK since 1983 or US since 1928 and show a notice on the plan.
- **UK plans default to US history from 1928,** because UK history from 1983 is short and optimistic.
- Withdrawal timing at depletion and the last year of variable percentage withdrawal were corrected.
