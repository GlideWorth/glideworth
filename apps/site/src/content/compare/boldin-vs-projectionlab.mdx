## The short version

- **Boldin** is a US-focused planner with connected bank accounts on its paid plan, a built-in AI assistant and an
  optional paid session with a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER professional
  ([pricing](https://boldin.com/pricing/), read 16 Nov 2026). Its help pages say it is built on US rules and cannot
  apply other countries' tax rules
  ([help](https://help.boldin.com/en/articles/6509299-is-plannerplus-suitable-for-canadians-or-other-non-us-citizens-planning-for-retirement),
  16 Nov 2026).
- **ProjectionLab** is a planner with a free tier that includes Monte Carlo and historical backtesting, tax
  presets for several countries, and no linked accounts required
  ([pricing](https://projectionlab.com/pricing), [home page](https://projectionlab.com/),
  [security](https://projectionlab.com/security), 16 Nov 2026).

## Which one suits whom

**Boldin may suit you if** you live in the United States, want balances to update from your institutions, and
like the option of paying for a session with a planner.

**ProjectionLab may suit you if** you want Monte Carlo and historical backtesting without paying, plan with one of
its country presets, or prefer not to link your bank.

**On price**, a year of Boldin PlannerPlus is $168 and a year of ProjectionLab Premium is $129, from the pricing
pages above. Both have a free tier, and they include different things.
