title: Today's money, several currencies, sharing and check-ins
date: 2026-11-16
tags: [engine, api, mcp, web, import]
---

- **Today's-money convention:** every amount carries a basis, and results can be shown in today's money or in
  future money without changing the plan.
- **Multi-currency:** accounts, property, debts and events in their own currency, with exchange-rate pairs and
  annual drift.
- **Inflation by category** (healthcare, education, housing or your own) and **debt payoff strategies**
  (avalanche and snowball).
- **Bank statement import** for OFX, QFX and QIF files, with a dry run and a reconciliation before anything is
  applied.
- **Share links:** expiring, revocable, read-only snapshots of a plan's results, with amounts shown in full, as
  percentages or hidden.
- **Scheduled check-ins** that re-run a plan and raise notifications when the chance of success, a milestone or
  net worth moves materially.
- **OAuth 2.1 for remote MCP clients,** so an assistant can connect by URL instead of a pasted key.
- A round of fixes from quality review.
