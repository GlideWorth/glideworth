The **Analysis** group in the sidebar holds the result pages: Results, Chance of success, Cash flow, Taxes,
Compare, Insights, Report, Estate and legacy, and Custom charts. Use the "Today's money" and "Future money"
switch in the top bar to change every figure's view.

**Before you start:** a plan that the engine can run. If it cannot, the page says "The plan could not be
simulated" and says why.

## Results (the projection)

Net worth year by year, the dates your milestones are reached, and any year in which money runs short. Open a
year for its drill-down: income, outflows, saving, withdrawals by account, investment return and the change in net
worth, as sentences and tables.

Every year's drill-down ends with a reconciliation, "this adds up": money in equals money out, each account's
opening balance plus its flows and growth equals its closing balance, and last year's net worth plus the changes
equals this year's. In today's money an **Inflation** line shows the buying power the year's money lost.

### Partial years

The plan starts in the month your balances are dated and ends at its horizon, so the first and last years often
cover fewer than 12 months. Their income, spending and taxes look low next to full years, so they are labelled,
for example "2026 (Nov-Mar, 5 months)", and charts and tables mark them "2026*". The **Actual months** and
**Annualised** switch scales a partial year's money moved to a full year. Balances, rates, totals over several
years and CSV exports always use actual months.

## Cash flow

Where money comes from and goes, as a Sankey diagram, for **One year** or a **Range**, **Grouped** or showing
**Every line**.

## Taxes

Tax by year, by jurisdiction and by kind, with effective and marginal rates, and income by tax band. These are
estimates. For a plan with accounts, property or income in another country, the page first lists what the estimate
leaves out, then shows tax by country and by reason (residence, source, citizenship, relief). Each rule has a "How
this is taxed, and why" explanation with its source. See [Tax settings, countries and
currency](/docs/planning/tax-and-currency/).

## Chance of success

The share of replays in which every year's spending was fully funded, with panels:

- **Settings**: trials, seed, historical data set, and what "success" means. The method in use is shown as
  "Market paths: Historical, random start years (default)"; open **Advanced** to choose another.
- **Failures**, with "When failed trials run short".
- **Sequence of returns**, with "Early returns vs success".
- **Milestones**, with "When milestones happen across trials".
- **Trial explorer**, with "Inspect one simulated future": scrub through trials ranked by ending net worth.

Method details are on [Monte Carlo and historical replay](/docs/methodology/monte-carlo/).

## Estate and legacy

What the estate is made of, by account type, and the net legacy to heirs at a chosen year of death, after estate
tax where the plan models it.

## Compare

Two or more plans side by side on net worth at end, peak net worth, retirement, financial independence, first
shortfall, unfunded spending, lifetime spending, lifetime taxes, average savings rate and first withdrawal rate.
"Where the difference comes from" attributes the change to individual edits.

## What this does not do

A result is a model of your inputs, not a prediction. Read [Known limitations](/docs/methodology/known-limitations/)
before you act on a figure.

<Callout type="warning" title="Estimates, not tax returns">
  Tax figures are estimates built from stated tax-year tables and simplified rules. They are not a tax return
  and not advice.
</Callout>
