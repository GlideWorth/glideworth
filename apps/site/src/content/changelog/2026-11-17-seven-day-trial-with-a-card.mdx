---
title: The free trial is now 7 days and starts with a card
date: 2026-11-17
tags: [web]
---

- **New Glideworth Cloud workspaces start with a 7-day free trial.** You add a card on Stripe's secure page to
  start it. Nothing is charged during the trial; the card is charged on day 8 for the plan you chose ($9 a month or
  $90 a year, in USD) unless you cancel before then in Billing.
- **A reminder email** goes out a few days before the first charge, with the date, the amount and how to cancel.
- **One free trial per person.** If you have had one before, or subscribe again after cancelling, the first
  payment is taken when you subscribe.
- People you invite into your workspace never need a card; your subscription covers them.
