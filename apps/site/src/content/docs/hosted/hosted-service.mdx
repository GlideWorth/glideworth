## Price

The price is {formatPrice(pricing.monthly)} a month or {formatPrice(pricing.yearly)} a year ({pricing.currency}),
after a {pricing.trialDays}-day free trial. The trial starts when you confirm your email and needs no card. When it
ends, the workspace becomes read-only (you can still open your plans, simulate and export) until you subscribe in
Billing; nothing is charged by itself. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for the current terms.

## What stays the same

- The self-hosted build is the whole product. There is no licence key and no feature that only the hosted
  service has.
- The source code is AGPL-3.0-only.
- Your data can leave: export a household as JSON at any time, and import it into a self-hosted instance. See
  [Glideworth JSON](/docs/importing/glideworth-json/).

## What this page does not say

It does not promise uptime or certifications. Those are stated where they are true.
