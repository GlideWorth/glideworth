- The application is licensed **AGPL-3.0-only** and the importers package **MIT**. Self-hosted builds are the whole
  product: no licence key and no telemetry.
- A security policy with a private reporting route, a self-hosting guide, contribution rules (including how to
  update a tax year with official sources) and a code of conduct.
- A contributor agreement and a trademark policy. Neither has been reviewed by a lawyer, and the legal entity
  behind the project is Web Ninja FZCO, a free zone company in Dubai.
- The source repository is public: https://github.com/GlideWorth/glideworth.
