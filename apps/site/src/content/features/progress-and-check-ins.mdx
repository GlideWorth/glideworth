A plan is a prediction, and the useful question a year later is whether reality is following it. Glideworth keeps
two records for that: **progress snapshots** of what you actually have, and a **plan health history** of what the
plan now says.

## Progress

Each update to your balances can record a snapshot of net worth. The app overlays actual against projected, with
per-account history that you can edit, and the dashboard shows net worth over a chosen range. Snapshots come from
four places:

- **By hand:** update balances in the app. At most one automatic snapshot is recorded per day, and a second update
  the same day changes that day's snapshot rather than adding another. A snapshot you recorded yourself is never
  overwritten by an automatic one.
- **From a script:** `POST /api/v1/households/{id}/balances` updates accounts, property values, mortgage and loan
  balances and debts in one all-or-nothing call, with an idempotency key so a retried sync is harmless.
- **From a file:** CSV balance and history imports, and bank statement files, reconcile against your current
  finances first (see [importing](/features/import-and-export/)).
- **Backfilled history:** a bulk call loads past snapshots.

Writes that move no balance, such as renames, never snapshot. A setting turns automatic snapshots off.

## Check-ins

A check-in re-runs a plan: the deterministic projection, a [Monte Carlo](/features/monte-carlo/) chance of success
and the [assumption audit](/features/explanations-and-audit/). Each run is one row of the plan's health history:
chance of success, financial-independence year, actual net worth against planned, end net worth, depletion year,
milestone dates and open findings. You can check in on demand, or let a built-in scheduler do it for the main active
plan of every household, daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly (weekly by default). The first run is a baseline.

A change is **material**, and raises a notification, when:

- the chance of success moves by more than a threshold (5 percentage points by default);
- a milestone slips later by a threshold (6 months by default) or is no longer reached;
- the audit finds a new warning or critical issue; or
- your net worth is more than a threshold (10% by default) below what the previous check-in projected for today.

You can change every threshold. "What changed since my last check-in?" is a single question: material changes, the
edits made since and by whom, and how net worth has moved.

## A worked illustration

An illustrative quarterly check-in on a made-up plan:

| | Last check-in | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chance of success | 86% | 79% |
| Retirement milestone | March 2038 | March 2038 |
| Net worth against plan | on plan | 12% behind |
| Open audit findings | 1 | 1 |

That raises two notifications, one for the 7-point fall in the chance of success and one for being 12% behind the
plan, and fires a `checkin.completed` webhook and a `notification.created` webhook for each. The "what changed"
answer lists the plan edits made since the last check-in and who made them, because the plan history records the
actor.

## Notifications and webhooks

Notifications appear in the app's notification centre and over the API (`GET /api/v1/notifications`). For
automation, the signed webhooks `checkin.completed` and `notification.created` carry the figures a script needs. A
webhook can post to a chat tool or a home server. See [the API](/features/rest-api/).

## What it does not do

- **No email in the self-hosted app.** Check-ins notify in the app and by webhook; you can point a webhook at
  anything that sends email. Email delivery is planned for the hosted service and not built yet.
- **It runs only while the server runs.** The scheduler is an in-process timer, not an external job service.
- **"Behind plan" is approximate:** the previous check-in's projection is interpolated across the year, in
  today's money.
- **No live balance sync.** Integrations with budgeting tools such as Lunch Money, YNAB, Monarch and Actual are
  on the [roadmap](/roadmap/); today you push balances from a script or import files. There are no bank connections.
- **Plans are not re-run on every edit.** Results are produced when a run starts or a check-in is due.
- **There is no completeness score** for how thoroughly you have filled in your finances.
