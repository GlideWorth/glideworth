---
title: The first complete planner
date: 2026-11-15
tags: [engine, tax, api, mcp, web, import]
---

- **A year-by-year engine** with a full ledger of income, tax, withdrawals and growth, for one person or a couple,
  with real assets, loans and survivor transfer.
- **Tax estimates** by country as plug-ins, each with a written list of what it leaves out.
- **Monte Carlo** over historical and random returns, seeded, with failure analysis and milestone timing.
- **Solvers and insights:** maximum spending, earliest retirement, required savings, safe withdrawal rate,
  guardrails, explanations, an assumption audit, branches and diffs.
- **A REST API** described in OpenAPI 3.1, and an **MCP server** that is read-only by default, with edits as
  change sets.
- **Importers** for ProjectionLab JSON and CSV, and a web app for editing plans and reading results.
