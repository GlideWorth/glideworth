A sub-processor is a company that handles personal data for us when we provide the service. We list them so you
can see who might handle your data. See the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) and the [data processing
agreement](/legal/dpa/). {site.entity.name}, the company that runs the service, is in the United Arab Emirates and
its staff may reach data from there (privacy policy, section 6); it is not a sub-processor.

Where a provider is outside the UK and the EU, we will use it only where a lawful transfer mechanism applies,
and the final list will say which (privacy policy, section 6).

## Not sub-processors

- **GitHub and the container registry** handle source code and software images, not customer data.
- **Let's Encrypt** receives our public host names to issue security certificates.
- **Your own AI assistant provider**, if you connect one, is your service, not ours. It receives what its tools
  return. See the privacy policy, section 9.

## Fallback and changes

Hetzner Online GmbH was considered as the hosting provider and is **not in use**; it would be added to this list
before any customer data moved to it. Vercel sees requests for our host names, as any DNS provider does, but not
customer content. Mailgun is supported by the software as an alternative email sender and is **not in use**.

The marketing site may be served from the same server or from a static host. If a separate host is used, it will
be listed here.

## How we will tell you about changes

We will update this page and email account holders at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts
handling customer data. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, you may cancel, delete your data and
ask for a refund of the unused part of what you prepaid before the change takes effect. Customers with a data
processing agreement have the rights set out in it.
