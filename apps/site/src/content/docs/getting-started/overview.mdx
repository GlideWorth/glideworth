Glideworth answers one question: will my money last, and what can I change? You describe your
household, accounts, income, spending and milestones. It projects your finances decades ahead, in
today's money or in future money, and tells you your chance of success, why a plan fails when it does,
and what you could change. It is a planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice.

## What it does

- **Plans a household** of one or two people: accounts, real assets, debts, income, expenses, savings
  flows, milestones (retire at 60, mortgage-free, life expectancy), drawdown order and assumptions.
- **Projects year by year** with a full ledger of every money movement, then **replays the plan many
  times** against historical or random market returns to give a chance of success.
- **Estimates tax** for {facts.taxCountryCount} countries, or with flat or custom rates. Every country lists what its estimate
  leaves out. These are estimates, never a tax return.
- **Answers questions**: the most you can spend at a target chance of success, the earliest retirement
  date, the savings you need, a safe withdrawal rate, spending guardrails.
- **Explains itself**: why a plan fails, where the money went in a year, what an assumption audit finds.
- **Lets you experiment safely**: branches, version history with undo, what-if templates, survivor
  scenarios, and change sets you preview before applying.
- **Is open to software**: a REST API and an MCP server, so scripts and AI assistants can read plans, run
  simulations and propose edits.

## What it does not do

- It does not connect to your bank. Balances come from you, a CSV or bank file, or a script.
- It does not give advice. It does not know your circumstances, your tax residence in detail or your risk
  tolerance.
- It does not model anything inside a year. The engine steps one year at a time.
- It does not predict markets. A chance of success is a stress test against history, not a probability of
  the future.

The full list is on [Known limitations](/docs/methodology/known-limitations/).
