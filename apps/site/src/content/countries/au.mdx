Australia has a detailed model for resident individuals, with {facts.simplificationCount('au')} written simplifications shown beside your
tax results. New Australian households default to the local income year, 1 July to 30 June.

Australian tax rates and thresholds are set in law and are not indexed. Glideworth keeps the legislated amounts through
2027-28; later years carry the 2027-28 rates forward and index thresholds with the plan's threshold policy. The figures
were checked against the legislation on 19 November 2026.

## Income tax and the Medicare levy

For 2026-27 the resident rates are: nil to A$18,200; 15% to A$45,000; 30% to A$135,000; 37% to A$190,000;
45% above. In 2025-26 the first rate was 16%; for 2027-28 and later it is 14%, as legislated by the Treasury Laws
Amendment (More Cost of Living Relief) Act 2025. The Medicare levy is 2% of taxable income, phasing in from the
low-income threshold of A$28,011 for a single person (A$44,268 for seniors and pensioners, which the model applies from
67); these thresholds apply from 2025-26 under an Act of June 2026. The low income tax offset is A$700 for incomes up
to A$37,500, reducing to nil as income rises. Dividends carry franking credits at the 30% company rate. Capital gains
get a 50% discount, and all gains are treated as held over 12 months.

## Superannuation

Glideworth models accumulation-phase and pension-phase super accounts.

- Contributions tax of 15% on concessional contributions; Division 293 adds a further 15% above A$250,000.
- Concessional contributions cap of A$30,000 in 2025-26 and A$32,500 in 2026-27; non-concessional cap of A$120,000
  and A$130,000 respectively. The 2026-27 caps could not be confirmed on the ATO's site.
- The model uses one age, 60, both for when you can access super and for when benefits become tax-free. Below 60,
  the taxable component is taxed with a 15% offset. Pension-phase earnings are untaxed.
- Minimum annual pension drawdown: 4% under 65; 5% at 65 to 74; 6% at 75 to 79; 7% at 80 to 84; 9% at 85 to 89;
  11% at 90 to 94; 14% from 95.
- Division 296 tax, on earnings for total super balances over A$3 million from 2026-27, is not modelled.

Glideworth does not default a government age pension for Australian households; if you receive one, enter it as
income.

## Worked example

  or private cover issues. It is not a forecast or advice.

A resident has taxable income of A$80,000 in 2026-27.

- Income tax: nil on the first A$18,200; 15% × (A$45,000 − A$18,200 = A$26,800) = A$4,020; 30% × (A$80,000 −
  A$45,000 = A$35,000) = A$10,500. Total **A$14,520**.
- Low income tax offset: nil, because at A$80,000 the A$700 offset has fully reduced away.
- Medicare levy: 2% × A$80,000 = **A$1,600**, as income is above the point where the levy is fully charged.
- Income tax and levy together: A$14,520 + A$1,600 = **A$16,120**.

## What to treat with care

- The ATO's site refused automated access when the tables were checked, so the 2026-27 super caps (A$32,500 and
  A$130,000) are not confirmed from the ATO. The tax rates and Medicare thresholds were checked against the Acts.
- 2027-28 rates are legislated; later years are projected.
- Medicare levy uses the single low-income threshold for each person, so a family with children is shown a different
  figure from the real one.
