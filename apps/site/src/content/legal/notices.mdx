## The data

The calculations use historical market data and tax tables published by others. They are facts or open data with
their own conditions. Some conditions ask us to show a statement. We show these here and in the app's "About the
data" page.

- **UK public sector information (Office for National Statistics, Bank of England, HMRC and GOV.UK, Scottish
  Government).** Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0
  (licence text: nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
  Sources: Office for National Statistics (CPI, RPI); Bank of England (Bank Rate, gilt yields); HM Revenue &
  Customs and GOV.UK (UK tax rates); Scottish Government (Scottish rates).
- **Federal Reserve Board.** Exchange rates: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.10 Foreign
  Exchange Rates.
- **US Bureau of Labor Statistics.** US inflation: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index for All
  Urban Consumers (CPI-U), series CUUR0000SA0.
- **Damodaran.** US stock, bond and bill returns: Aswath Damodaran, Historical Returns on Stocks, Bonds and
  Bills, NYU Stern (pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar).
- **New Zealand Inland Revenue.** Contains material from Inland Revenue (New Zealand), Crown copyright, licensed
  under CC BY 4.0.
- **Spain.** Source: Agencia Tributaria and Seguridad Social (official websites). Their reuse conditions ask us to
  cite the source and not to distort the content.
- **France.** Source: impots.gouv.fr, service-public.gouv.fr, BOFiP-Impôts, with the date of last update, under
  the Licence Ouverte 2.0; URSSAF under the Open Database Licence.
- **US federal agencies (IRS, SSA, HHS).** Public domain. Source: IRS, SSA.

Tax tables for other countries are transcribed from official publications. Each country page lists its sources.
The tax and market data are estimates for the years and periods stated. See the [disclaimer](/legal/disclaimer/).
