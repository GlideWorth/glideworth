date: 2026-11-23

---

- **Costs that follow your children's ages.** Add childcare, school, college or support straight from a dependant,
  with start and end ages. Costs move when a birth date changes, and can go into one plan or every plan at once.
- **ACA household size worked out for you.** It follows the people on your return and the dependants you list,
  year by year, so the subsidy estimate keeps up as children grow up. A size you type in still wins.
- **Balance history import.** Paste from a spreadsheet or upload CSV/TSV, match columns to accounts, check a
  cell-by-cell preview, then merge or replace. Ambiguous dates are never guessed. Export works the same way. Also
  in the API and through your AI assistant.
- **Hide amounts.** One switch (or ⇧H) masks every money figure so you can share your screen. Exports are never
  masked.
- **Long-term care** is its own expense type, and a **bonus can be a percentage of a salary**.
- **US tax:** tiered and fixed employer contributions, spousal IRA and traditional IRA deduction limits, and a
  fix so unborn children no longer count for the child tax credit.
- **More:** a Lunch Money CSV preset, Galicia's regional IRPF scale, confirmed Idaho and Australian figures, and
  quality fixes across the app.
- Saved documents move to schema version 6 on first load, with nothing rewritten. Builds older than 1.1.0 cannot
  open a version 6 document, so upgrade every instance that shares a database.
