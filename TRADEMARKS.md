the service holds their financial plans.

## What you can do without asking

- **Say what your thing is based on or works with.** "Based on Glideworth", "a fork of Glideworth",
  "compatible with Glideworth", "imports Glideworth exports" and "works with the Glideworth API" are all
  fine, if true. Use plain text, not the logo, and make clear that you are a separate project.
- **Write about Glideworth.** Reviews, tutorials, comparisons, articles, videos and talks may use the
  name and may show screenshots of the official software.
- **Run it for yourself.** Self-hosting for you, your family or your own organisation, under whatever
  hostname you like, needs no permission. The software's own screens may keep showing the name.
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** (for example official container images or packages)
  under the Glideworth name, so long as you do not change them and you say where they come from.
- **Link to the official site and repository.**

## What you cannot do without written permission

- **Name your product, service, company, app, package, container image, domain or social account
  "Glideworth"**, or anything confusingly similar (for example "Glideworth Cloud", "Glideword", "Glide

- **Use the logo or brand assets** in your own product or service, or in a way that looks like the
  official brand.
- **Run a hosted or managed service that presents itself as Glideworth**, or as the official or

  own name and brand, as long as you follow the AGPL, including publishing your changes.
- **Suggest endorsement, sponsorship, partnership or certification** by the Project when there is none.
- **Register or use the Marks** as a trademark, business name, domain, app-store listing or social-media

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version, or host one for other people:

1. choose your own name and remove the Glideworth name and logo from the screens, the page titles, the
   documentation, the images and the packages you publish (the licence and copyright notices must stay);
2. say clearly that your version is not the official Glideworth;
3. do not use wording or design that could make someone believe it is official. In particular do not ask
   people for data, passwords or payment under a name that looks like ours.

## Contributors

Contributing code does not give you any rights in the Marks. Contributors may say they contribute to
Glideworth.

## Asking permission, and reporting misuse

Write to `trademarks@glideworth.com` with what you want to do. Permission, if given, will
be in writing and may be limited in time or scope. To report a service that looks like an impersonation,
use the same address. If it also asks users for passwords or payment, report it to the hosting provider
and the registrar as well.
