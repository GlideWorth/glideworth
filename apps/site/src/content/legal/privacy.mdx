## 2. The short version

- We hold the plans and figures you enter so that we can show you projections. We do not use them for anything
  else. The hosted app's session recordings show the figures on your screen; we watch them only to improve the
  app and fix faults.
- We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising trackers. We do not connect to your banks.
- The official marketing website can load Google Tag Manager for page measurement, and only that website's production build does. It sets analytics cookies only if you accept them, and never advertising cookies. The app, the free calculators and a self-hosted copy do not load it.
- The hosted app sends product analytics, session recordings and error reports to PostHog: which pages and
  features you use, and recordings of what the app shows and how you move through it, linked to your account id,
  email address and name. Recordings include the figures on screen; what you type into form fields is masked.
  Self-hosted copies send nothing.
- Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. It is **not end-to-end encrypted**: our servers can read your
  plans. That is what lets scheduled check-ins and AI assistants you connect work.
- Glideworth runs no AI on your data. If you connect your own AI assistant, that assistant's provider receives
  what its tools return (see section 9).
- If you ask a free calculator to email you its result, we send that one email and keep your address only as
  long as section 7 says. We add you to our reminder and updates list only if you tick the box and then confirm
  by the link in that email, and every list email lets you unsubscribe in one click.
- We give everyone the data protection rights described in section 11, wherever you live.
- We do not keep data forever. Section 7 gives every retention period.
- You can export everything and delete your workspace yourself, in the app.

### 3.1 The logging rule

No financial value, plan content, household name, plan name, request body, query string, cookie, token or error
message text goes into our logs or error reports. Logs hold only an allowed list of fields: identifiers,
counts, durations, status codes, error names and codes, and route templates. A test in our build fails if a
sample amount or plan name shows up in captured logs. Web server access logs do include your IP address; section 7
says how long they are kept.

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers
(sub-processors) who process data on our instructions. They are listed, with what each one sees and where, on
the [sub-processor list](/legal/subprocessors/). Two of them also use some data for their own purposes, as
independent controllers under their own privacy policies: our payment provider (Stripe) for fraud prevention and
legal compliance, and our bot-check provider (Cloudflare) to improve its bot detection. We also share data where
the law requires it, and with a buyer
if the business is sold (you would be told first).

### 5.1 Signing in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. You can always use an email address and password instead. If you choose
"Continue with Google":

- **What we send to Google.** Only the sign-in request: our app's identifier at Google, the page on our site
  that Google returns you to, the kinds of information we ask for (your Google account's identifier, email
  address and name), and random values that protect the request against forgery. We send Google nothing else
  about you, and nothing from your workspace. The app does not load any Google script; the button is a plain link.
- **What Google learns.** Google sees that you are signing in to Glideworth, and when. Google handles that under
  its own privacy policy, as the provider of your Google account. For this, Google is not our sub-processor:
  it acts for you, not on our instructions.
- **What we receive and keep.** See the table in section 3. We use Google's identifier, not the email address,
  to recognise your Google account. We use what Google sends once to sign you in and then discard it, apart from
  that identifier, the email address at the time you linked the account, and the link and last-used dates.
- **Your existing account is protected.** If an account with the same email address already exists, signing in
  with Google does not open it. You sign in with your password first and link Google in your security settings.
- **Two-step sign-in still applies.** If you turned it on, we still ask for your code after Google.
- **Unlinking.** You can unlink Google in your security settings once your account has a password. Unlinking
  deletes the record of the link. Deleting your account deletes it too.

Our staff cannot see inside your workspace by default. The operator console shows account facts (email, plan,
status, sizes, counts) but not your households, plans, balances or results. The one exception is a **support
grant**: you can allow a named operator read-only access to your workspace for up to 7 days. Each view is
recorded and you are emailed when it is first used.

## 6. Where your data is, and who can reach it

**Where it is stored.** We store your data in the European Union: the main servers in the Netherlands and the
backups in Germany. Some providers are based in, or may process data in, other countries, notably the United States (for
example payments, email and bot checks). The [sub-processor list](/legal/subprocessors/) says where each provider is.

**Where we are.** Our company, and the people who run the service, are in the United Arab Emirates. They may
reach data stored in the EU from the UAE. They do so only for support and for operating the service, and only
under the access controls in section 8: the operator console is on a private interface with mandatory
two-factor sign-in, it does not show workspace contents, and the only route to your workspace contents is a
support grant that you create, which is logged and which you are told about.

**What protects it.** The EU has not made an adequacy decision for the UAE (we checked the European
Commission's list on 16 November 2026), and we do not rely on an adequacy decision from the UK or any other
country for the UAE. That means we cannot say the UAE is recognised as giving your data the same level of
protection as the EU or UK law does. We rely instead on the controls in this policy, the limits on who can reach
your data, and the contract terms we agree with providers. For each provider outside the UK and the EU, the
sub-processor list names the transfer safeguard that applies, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (for
certified providers) or standard contractual clauses, or says that the provider publishes none. UAE
data protection law may also apply to us, and nothing in this policy limits any right it gives you. You can ask
for a description of the safeguards at the contact address above.

## 8. How we protect it

- Encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Each workspace's backups are encrypted with a key of its own.
  Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Tokens, API keys and recovery codes are stored as hashes. Two-factor
  secrets are encrypted.
- **Not end-to-end encrypted.** Our servers can read your plans. We have chosen not to claim otherwise.
- Each workspace is its own database file, so one customer's data is not mixed into another's.
- Sign-in throttling, bot checks on sign-up, re-confirmation of your password before sensitive actions, session
  lists you can revoke, and alerts when a new device signs in.
- Staff access to workspace contents only through your support grant.
- A logging rule that keeps financial values out of logs and error reports (section 3.1).

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay,
and we will tell the data protection authority where the law requires it. Report a vulnerability to
<a href={`mailto:${site.contact.security}`}>{site.contact.security}</a> (see [security](/security/)).

## 9. AI assistants you connect

Glideworth has a REST API and an MCP server. You can connect an AI assistant of your choice with a key whose
scopes you set. Writes through MCP are **off by default** for new workspaces; you turn them on.

- **Glideworth runs no AI on your data.** We do not train models on it and we do not send it to an AI provider.
- When your assistant calls a tool, the tool's answer goes to the assistant, and so to whoever runs it. That
  provider's own privacy terms apply to what it receives. If that matters, use a local model.
- We cannot see or control what your assistant does with the data. You can revoke a key or connection at any
  time in the app.

## 11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to: give you access to your data; correct it; delete it; restrict how we use
it; give you a portable copy; and stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. These are the rights in
the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, and we give them to everyone. You also keep any rights your own country's law gives you.
You can withdraw consent at any time: for the calculator reminder and updates list, with the one-click unsubscribe
link in every list email, or by writing to us.

You can do most of this yourself:

- **Export.** The workspace owner can download a complete copy of the workspace as a database file that opens
  in the self-hosted app. Each household also has a JSON export. A separate download gives you the account data
  we hold about you (profile, memberships, linked sign-in methods such as Google, sessions and security events).
- **Delete.** The workspace owner can delete the workspace, and you can delete your user account, in the app.
  Section 7 says what happens next.
- **Change** your name, email and sign-in settings in the app, including linking or unlinking Google.

For anything else, email <a href={`mailto:${site.contact.privacy}`}>{site.contact.privacy}</a>. We will answer
within one month and we do not charge for this. We may need to check who you are first. If you are a member of
someone else's workspace (for example a household member or an adviser's client), the workspace owner may be the
right person to ask, and we will tell you if so.

## 12. Complaints

You can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk). In the EU it is the authority in the country where you live, work or think a breach happened.
Elsewhere, it is the authority for your country or region. We would like a chance to put things right first, so
please contact us, but you do not have to.

## 13. Children

Glideworth is for adults. You must be 18 or over to have an account, and we ask you to confirm it when you sign
up. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will
delete it.

## 14. If you live in the United States

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those
terms are used in US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. The categories in section 3
are the categories we collect. We give you the rights in section 11 whichever state you live in, including the
rights to know, delete, correct and get a copy of your data, and we do not treat you worse for using them. Use the
contact address above. You may appeal a refusal by replying to it.

## 15. Changes

We will update this policy when what we do changes. If a change matters to you, for example a new
sub-processor, we will email account holders at least 30 days beforehand. The date at the top shows the
latest version.
