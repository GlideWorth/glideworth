## What Glideworth is

Glideworth is a calculator. You enter your situation and your assumptions. It works out what follows from them:
how your money might last, what tax you might pay, how a change might affect the result. The numbers depend on
what you put in. Different inputs give different answers.

## What the results are

- **Projections are estimates.** They show one possible set of outcomes from your assumptions. Monte Carlo
  results show a range of outcomes from historical data and random draws. A "chance of success" is a model
  output. It is not a probability that your own future will go a certain way.
- **The past does not predict the future.** Historical returns, inflation and exchange rates in the tool come from
  published datasets for the periods stated. Markets, tax and inflation can behave differently from anything in
  the data.
- **Tax figures are estimates.** The tax models cover {facts.taxCountryCount} countries, for the tax years stated
  on each [country page](/planning/). Each model is simplified and leaves things out. They are not tax
  returns, not tax advice, and not a substitute for a tax professional or the tax authority's own rules. Tax
  law changes, and so can the tables in the tool.
- **Errors are possible.** We test the software and publish what it does and does not model, but it can contain
  mistakes. Check results that matter against official sources.

## Things that may look like recommendations

Some features point out what to look at or try, for example the assumption check, tax strategy comparison,
conversion analysis, spending guardrails, and the prompts offered to AI assistants. Where they use words such as
"fix", "best", "recommended" or "what to do", those words describe a change to **your own inputs** and what
**the model** then shows. They are generic. They do not take account of your whole situation, they do not name
any particular fund, security, product or provider, and they are not a recommendation to act. The service gives
no personal recommendation to you and no recommendation of any investment, product or provider.

## AI assistants

If you connect an AI assistant through MCP, what it says is its own output, shaped by its provider and by what it
decides to do with the data. It may be wrong. Glideworth runs no AI on your data and does not control what a
connected assistant says. Treat it as you would any other source: check it.
