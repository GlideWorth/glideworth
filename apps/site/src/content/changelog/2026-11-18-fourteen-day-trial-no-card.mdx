title: The free trial is now 14 days, with no card
date: 2026-11-18
tags: [web]

- **New Glideworth Cloud workspaces start a 14-day free trial as soon as you confirm your email.** You go straight
  into the planner with every feature, and we do not ask for a card.
- **When the trial ends, nothing is charged.** Your workspace becomes read-only: your plans stay there, and you can
  still open them, run simulations and export your data. Subscribe at any time ($9 a month or $90 a year, in USD)
  and editing comes back at once.
- **Subscribe during the trial and you keep the days you have left.** With more than two days to go, the first
  charge falls on the day the trial would have ended.
- **Reminder emails** go out a few days before the trial ends and once it has ended.
- **Signed up in the last day but never started the card trial?** Your 14 days start now.
- One free trial per person. People you invite into your workspace never need a card.
