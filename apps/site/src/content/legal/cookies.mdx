## 2. The hosted app (app.glideworth.com)

### Cookies

All of these are set by our own server, only for app.glideworth.com, and only over HTTPS (`__Host-` cookies,
Secure). None of them is used for tracking or advertising.

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-gw_session` | Keeps you signed in. Holds a random token; the server stores only a hash of it | Up to 30 days. The server ends the session sooner after 7 days without use, or when you sign out | Strictly necessary. HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict |
| `__Host-gw_csrf` | A second token the app sends back with every change, to stop other websites making changes for you | Same as the session | Strictly necessary. SameSite=Strict. Readable by the app's own script, by design |
| `__Host-gw_device` | Recognises a browser you have signed in from before, so that a sign-in from a new device is recorded in your security log and, if you keep sign-in alerts on, emailed to you. Holds a random value; the server stores only a keyed hash of it | Up to 400 days | Security only. HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict |
| `__Host-gw_signup` | Set when you sign up. Lets the verification link sign you in only in the browser where you chose your password; in any other browser you must enter the password too | Up to 48 hours, as long as the verification link | Security only. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, so it is sent when you arrive from your email |

**Only if you use "Continue with Google":**

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-gw_oidc` | Ties a Google sign-in you start to your browser, so that someone else's Google sign-in cannot be finished in your browser, and yours cannot be finished in theirs. Holds a random value; the server keeps the details of the sign-in, encrypted | 10 minutes at most. Deleted when Google sends you back | Strictly necessary for a sign-in you asked for. HttpOnly. SameSite=Lax, because it must be sent when Google returns you to us |
| `__Host-gw_oidc_signup` | Set only if you are new. Lets your browser finish creating the account (name, age confirmation, terms) after Google | 15 minutes at most. Deleted when the account is created | Strictly necessary. HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict |
| `__Host-gw_oidc_link` | Set only when you link Google to an existing account from your security settings. Holds the pending link until you confirm it | 10 minutes at most | Strictly necessary. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax |

Google sets its own cookies on its own pages, under its own policy. The app does not load anything from Google;
the button is a plain link.

### Local storage

The app stores these on your device. Apart from the PostHog identifier, none of them is sent to a third party. Some hold the figures you typed.

| Key | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `glideworth-ui` | Your display choices: theme, density, reduced motion |
| `glideworth-prefs` and `glideworth-chart-prefs` | Interface preferences, such as chart options and tour progress |
| `glideworth:settings:v1` | A local copy of your settings |
| `glideworth:drafts:v1:` followed by a household id | Unsaved edits to a plan, kept so you do not lose them if the page reloads or you go offline. This can contain figures you entered |
| `glideworth:chart-views:v1:` followed by a plan id | Chart views you saved |
| `glideworth:sample-doc:v1` | The sample household, if you used it |
| `ph_` followed by the PostHog project key | Hosted app only: PostHog's random identifier for your browser and its analytics settings, sent to PostHog with each analytics event (section 3) |
| `glideworth.handoff.v1` | Only if you came from one of the calculators on this website: the numbers you entered there, so the app can offer to start your plan with them. Removed once you use or decline them, and ignored after 7 days |

Older versions used the same keys starting with `cairn` (`cairn-ui`, `cairn:drafts:v1:` and so on). When the app
starts it copies each old key to its new name and removes the old one, so your settings and drafts carry over.

### Cache storage

The app registers a service worker that keeps a copy of the app's own files in your browser's cache, so the app
opens quickly and can load offline. It holds the program files, not your plans.

On a shared computer, use the "erase local data" control in settings, or clear your browser data, when you
finish.

### Third parties on app pages

- **Bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile).** On sign-up, password reset, and sign-in after several failed attempts,
  the app loads a bot-protection widget from Cloudflare (challenges.cloudflare.com) that checks you are a person.
  Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser signals. See the [sub-processor list](/legal/subprocessors/).
  In our check on 17 November 2026, loading the widget in a fresh browser set no cookies, for our site or for
  Cloudflare. Cloudflare's own [cookie policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/cookie-policy/) applies to anything it
  stores inside its widget; it lists its challenge cookies as strictly necessary. The widget is not loaded on other
  pages.
- **Payment pages.** When you subscribe or manage billing, you go to pages hosted by Stripe, our payment
  provider. Stripe sets its own cookies there, under its own policy.

## 4. If you run Glideworth yourself

The self-hosted app uses the same local storage and cache as above. Its sign-in cookies are named
`glideworth_session` and `glideworth_csrf` (formerly `cairn_session` and `cairn_csrf`, which it still accepts and
replaces), and it does not set the device, sign-up or Google cookies. It has no bot check. Its interactive API
reference at `/docs` loads its viewer from the jsDelivr content delivery network, so jsDelivr sees your IP address
when you open that page. The hosted service's API reference does not.

## 6. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser. If you block the sign-in cookies you cannot
use the app. The website works without any storage.
