A sub-processor is a company that handles personal data for us, on our instructions, when we provide the hosted
service at app.glideworth.com. This page lists every one we use today. See also the [privacy
policy](/legal/privacy/) and the [data processing agreement](/legal/dpa/).

{site.entity.name}, the company that runs the service, is in the United Arab Emirates and its staff may reach data
from there (privacy policy, section 6). It is not a sub-processor.

## Providers you choose to use

These are not our sub-processors. They act for you, under their own terms.

- **Google, if you choose "Continue with Google".** Google receives the sign-in request and tells us your Google
  account's identifier, email address and name. Google acts as an independent controller of your Google account
  under the [Google APIs terms](https://developers.google.com/terms) and its own [privacy
  policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Signing in with Google is optional. See the privacy policy,
  section 5.1.
- **Your own AI assistant provider**, if you connect one. It receives what its tools return. See the privacy
  policy, section 9.

## Not sub-processors

- **GitHub** holds the source code and builds the software images. It does not receive customer data.
- **Let's Encrypt** receives our public host names to issue security certificates.
- **Google Tag Manager** runs on the official marketing website (glideworth.com) for page measurement. It receives
  a visitor's IP address, the page address and browser details, not account or planning data. Analytics
  cookies are set only if the visitor accepts them; advertising storage is never granted. The app, the free calculators and a self-hosted copy of the website do not load it.

The app uses no advertising service.

## How we tell you about changes

We will update this page and email account holders at least 30 days before a new or replacement sub-processor
starts handling customer data. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, you may stop using the
service, delete your data and receive a refund of the unused part of what you prepaid. Customers covered by the
[data processing agreement](/legal/dpa/) have the rights in its section 5. The date at the top of this page shows
the latest version.
