date: 2026-11-16

- **United Arab Emirates** added as the twelfth country: no personal income tax, with the
  end-of-service gratuity, the DEWS and savings schemes, and GPSSA pensions (Law 57 of 2023).
- **Cross-border residency** in the engine: a residency timeline per person with split years
  prorated by month, tax on income sourced in a country you have left, US citizenship-based
  taxation with the foreign earned income exclusion and foreign tax credit, treaty relief by credit
  (UK-UAE from the convention; others basic), departure tax in Canada and Australia, and the UK's
  temporary non-residence rules.
- **API, MCP and web:** a residency timeline editor, a country on every account, property, debt and income, tax
  shown by country and reason, a "how is this taxed and why" explanation, a relocation what-if and a stay-or-move
  comparison. The screens are new.
- Plans that stay in one country give byte-identical results to the previous engine version.
- Residence is what you enter: no statutory residence test is applied.
