Spain is estimated at a **basic level**: the main rules are in, with {facts.simplificationCount('es')} written
simplifications, and it has had less checking than the United Kingdom or the United States. Every figure below was
checked against the BOE, the tax agency (AEAT) and the Seguridad Social on 19 November 2026.

The state income tax scales and minimums are the law in force for both 2025 and 2026. The regional scales are the
2025 tables, the latest the tax agency has published, and are used for 2026 as well: the 2026 regional scales could not
be confirmed. Later years carry the tables forward with the plan's threshold policy. Social security uses 2026 rates.
The model covers the territorio común, not Navarra or the País Vasco.

**Which region is assumed.** Set your region in the plan to use its scale. Madrid, Cataluña, Andalucía and Comunitat
Valenciana have their own scales. Any other region, or a plan with no region, is assumed to have a regional scale
equal to the state scale. That is a simplification, not any region's real scale.

## What is modelled

- **State general scale**: 9.5% to €12,450; 12% to €20,200; 15% to €35,200; 18.5% to €60,000; 22.5% to €300,000;
  24.5% above. Each region applies its own scale to the other half of the tax.
- **Savings scale** (2025 and 2026): each half runs 9.5% to €6,000; 10.5% to €50,000; 11.5% to €200,000; 13.5% to
  €300,000; 15% above, so the combined scale runs from 19% to 30%.
- **Minimums**: personal €5,550, with €1,150 more from age 65 and €1,400 more from 75; descendants under 25 €2,400,
  €2,700, €4,000 and €4,500, and €2,800 more for a child under three.
- **Work income**: €2,000 of other expenses, and a reduction for low earners. The reduction is measured on net work
  income after social security but before the €2,000: €7,302 up to €14,852, tapering to nothing at €19,747.50.
- **Social security (employee)**: 6.5% (4.70% contingencias comunes, 1.55% unemployment, 0.10% training and 0.15% for
  the intergenerational equity mechanism, MEI) up to a maximum base of €5,101.20 a month.
- **Pension plans** (planes de pensiones): contributions reduce the taxable base up to €1,500 a year, or 30% of net work
  and business income if lower. Withdrawals are taxed as work income. The default state pension age is 67; you enter
  the pension amount yourself.
- **Wealth tax and solidarity tax** (off by default): net worth is split evenly between the people in the household.
  The wealth tax uses the state scale above a €700,000 exemption per person, except in Cataluña (its own scale, €500,000
  exempt) and Comunitat Valenciana (its own scale, €1,000,000 exempt). The solidarity tax on large fortunes applies to
  wealth above €3,000,000 after a €700,000 exemption (1.7%, 2.1% and 3.5%), and only where it is larger than the wealth
  tax. Madrid and Andalucía have a 100% wealth tax bonus, but while the solidarity tax is in force the bonus only removes
  the wealth tax above it, so residents there pay the solidarity tax amount.

## Worked example

<Callout type="note" title="Illustrative">
  Made-up figures, to show the arithmetic with the state scale and 2026 social security, for a person in a region with
  no scale of its own. It is not a forecast or advice.
</Callout>

An employee has a gross salary of €30,000.

- Social security: 6.5% × €30,000 = **€1,950**.
- Net work income before other expenses: €30,000 − €1,950 = €28,050. This is above €19,747.50, so no low-earner
  reduction applies. After the €2,000 other expenses: **€26,050**.
- State scale on €26,050: 9.5% × €12,450 = €1,182.75; 12% × €7,750 = €930.00; 15% × (€26,050 − €20,200 = €5,850) =
  €877.50. Total €2,990.25.
- Less the scale applied to the personal minimum: 9.5% × €5,550 = €527.25. State half of the tax: **€2,463.00**.
- The regional half uses the same scale in a region with no scale of its own: another **€2,463.00**.
- Income tax: €2,463.00 + €2,463.00 = **€4,926.00**.

## Historical data for simulations

No Spanish or euro-area market history is bundled. Spanish households are tested against US history by default
(S&P 500, 10-year Treasuries, T-bills and CPI-U, 1928 to 2025, in US dollars).

## What to treat with care

- The 2026 regional scales could not be confirmed, so the 2025 ones are used. Only four regions have their own.
- Spain does not index its scales by law; later years follow the plan's threshold policy.
- Only individual returns are modelled, and regional minimums and deductions are not.
- Descendant minimums are split evenly between two parents and count dependants under 25.
- The wealth and solidarity taxes use period-start net worth and ignore the main-home exemption. The 2026 regional
  wealth tax rules could not be confirmed, so the 2025 rules are used.
- No preset has been checked line by line against an official calculator for every case.
