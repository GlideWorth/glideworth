There are two ways to share a plan: give a person a sign-in, or send a link to the results.

## Roles

One instance can have several logins. The first, the **owner**, is created at first-run setup.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including users, webhooks, OAuth clients and the audit log. |
| Editor (a partner) | Read, simulate and write: plans, finances, imports, share links, the check-in schedule and their own API keys. Not users, webhooks or the audit log. |
| Viewer (an adviser) | Read and simulate only. Every route and MCP tool that writes is refused or absent. |

Roles are instance-wide, not per household. A role caps every credential of that person: browser session, API key
and OAuth token.

### Invite someone

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings**, then **People &amp; access**.
2. Invite a person with a name and a role (editor or viewer). You get a one-time invite token to give them,
   for example inside a link.
3. They open the link, choose a passphrase, and are signed in. You never learn their passphrase. The invite works
   once and expires after 7 days.
</Steps>

You can change a role, disable a person, remove them, or issue a passphrase reset. The **Audit log** under Settings
shows who changed what, including writes by API keys and MCP tools.

## Share links

A share link shows the results of one plan to someone without an account.

<Steps>
1. In **Settings**, open **Share links**, or use the share control for a plan.
2. Choose a label, how long it lasts ("Expires after", 1 to 365 days, default 30), how money is shown ("Money shown
   as"), and whether to "Include the chance of success".
3. Copy the link. The token is shown once.
</Steps>

Money can be shown in full, as a percentage of your starting net worth, or hidden entirely. The link serves a
snapshot of results taken when it was created (you can refresh it). It never contains your household, people,
accounts, balances, settings or other plans, and life-expectancy milestones are left out because they name a
person. Revoke a link and it stops working at once.

## What this does not do

- It does not give per-household access control. Roles are instance-wide.
- A share link is not an API credential: it opens no other route.
- Shared pages are not indexed by search engines.
