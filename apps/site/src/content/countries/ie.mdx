Ireland is estimated at a **basic level**: the main rules are in, with {facts.simplificationCount('ie')} written
simplifications, and it has had less checking than the United Kingdom or the United States. The tables are for 2026, and
later years index them with the plan's threshold policy.

## What is modelled

- **Income tax (2026)**: 20% up to the rate band and 40% above. The band is €44,000 for a single person, €48,000 for a single parent,
  and €53,000 for a married couple, with up to €35,000 more for a second income. Credits: personal €2,000 (single)
  or €4,000 (married), employee €2,000, earned income €2,000, and an age credit of €245 (€490 married) from 65.
- **USC**: 0.5% to €12,012; 2% to €28,700; 3% to €70,044; 8% above; not payable when total income is €13,000 or less.
  State Pension and other social welfare income are exempt.
- **PRSI**: Class A employee at an average of about 4.24% for 2026 (4.2% January to September and 4.35% from 1 October).
  No employee PRSI at €352 a week or less, and none from age 66.
- **DIRT** at 33% on deposit interest. **Capital gains tax** on most gains at 33% for disposals to 6 October 2026 and 31% from 7 October 2026, with a
  €1,270 annual exemption. Glideworth has no disposal dates, so 2026 gains use the average by days (32.53%) and later
  years 31%.
- **Pension relief** is age-related: 15% of earnings up to age 29, 20% from 30, 25% from 40, 30% from 50, 35% from 55 and 40% from
  60, on earnings capped at €115,000. Pension access is from 60. There is no USC or PRSI relief on contributions.

## Worked example

<Callout type="note" title="Illustrative">
  Made-up figures, to show the arithmetic with the 2026 table. It assumes a single PAYE employee with no
  pension contributions. It is not a forecast or advice.
</Callout>

A single employee earns €50,000 in 2026.

- Income tax: 20% × €44,000 = €8,800; 40% × (€50,000 − €44,000 = €6,000) = €2,400. Total €11,200. Less credits of
  €2,000 (personal) + €2,000 (employee) = €4,000. Income tax **€7,200**.
- USC: 0.5% × €12,012 = €60.06; 2% × (€28,700 − €12,012 = €16,688) = €333.76; 3% × (€50,000 − €28,700 = €21,300) =
  €639.00. Total **€1,032.82**.
- PRSI: €50,000 × 4.2375% (the 2026 average of 4.2% and 4.35%) = **€2,118.75**.
- Together: €7,200 + €1,032.82 + €2,118.75 = **€10,351.57**.

## Historical data for simulations

No Irish or euro-area market history is bundled. Irish households are tested against US history by default
(S&P 500, 10-year Treasuries, T-bills and CPI-U, 1928 to 2025, in US dollars).

## What to treat with care

- Capital gains tax for 2026 is an average of the 33% and 31% rates (32.53%), because the model does not know when
  in the year a gain is made. Development land (33%) and the other special CGT rates are not modelled.
- PRSI uses an average of the two 2026 rates.
- To model the 25% tax-free retirement lump sum, set a tax-free withdrawal share on the account.
- No preset has been checked line by line against an official calculator for every case.
