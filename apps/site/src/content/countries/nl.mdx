The Netherlands is estimated at a **basic level**: the main rules are in, with {facts.simplificationCount('nl')} written
simplifications, and it has had less checking than the United Kingdom or the United States. The tables are for 2026. Some
figures are provisional (see below), and later years index the 2026 tables with the plan's threshold policy.

## What is modelled

- **Box 1** for 2026, under AOW age: 35.75% on income up to €38,883, 37.56% up to €78,426 and 49.50% above. These
  rates include the national insurance premiums. From AOW age the first rate is 17.85%.
- **Tax credits**: the general tax credit (maximum €3,115 below AOW age, reducing above €29,736), the employment tax
  credit (maximum about €5,685, reducing at higher incomes and nil from €132,920), the older-person credit
  (up to €2,067) and a single-person older-person credit of €540.
- **Box 2** (substantial interest): 24.5% up to €68,843 and 31% above, applied only to income you tag as such.
- **Box 3**: a deemed return on assets instead of tax on actual interest, dividends or gains. The allowance is €59,357
  per person (€118,714 with a partner), the deemed returns are 1.28% on savings, 6.00% on other assets and 2.70% on
  debts (above a €3,800 threshold), and the rate is 36%. The savings and debt percentages are provisional.
- **AOW age** in the model is 67 for people born before 1961 and 67 years and 3 months for those born later. You enter
  the pension amount yourself.

## Worked example

<Callout type="note" title="Illustrative">
  Made-up figures, to show the arithmetic with the 2026 table. It assumes a person below AOW age whose
  box 1 income is all earned, with no other deductions. It is not a forecast or advice.
</Callout>

A person has €30,000 of box 1 income from work in 2026.

- Box 1 tax and premiums: 35.75% × €30,000 = **€10,725.00**.
- General tax credit: €3,115 − 6.398% × (€30,000 − €29,736 = €264) = €3,115 − €16.89 = **€3,098.11**.
- Employment tax credit: €5,300 + 1.95% × (€30,000 − €25,845 = €4,155) = €5,300 + €81.02 = **€5,381.02**.
- Tax after credits: €10,725.00 − €3,098.11 − €5,381.02 = **€2,245.87**.

The model does not split the credits between the tax and premium parts, so it treats the credits as fully usable.

## Historical data for simulations

No Dutch or euro-area market history is bundled. Dutch households are tested against US history by default (S&P 500,
10-year Treasuries, T-bills and CPI-U, 1928 to 2025, in US dollars).

## What to treat with care

- The 2026 box 3 deemed returns for savings and debts are the provisional figures used for the provisional
  assessment; final figures are set after the year ends. The counter-evidence route is not applied.
- Box 3 assets come from period-start wealth: savings, taxable investments (including investment property) and debts
  other than the main-home mortgage. Pensions are excluded. If the engine cannot split them, the whole portfolio is
  treated as other assets.
- Box 3 replaces tax on actual interest, dividends and gains, so they are not taxed again.
- The year you reach AOW age is taxed at the AOW rate for the whole year if you reach it by 1 January, otherwise at
  the under-AOW rate.
- No preset has been checked line by line against an official calculator for every case.
