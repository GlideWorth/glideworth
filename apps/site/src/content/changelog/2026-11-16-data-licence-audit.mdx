title: Data licence audit and a new UK history
date: 2026-11-16
tags: [docs, engine]
---

- Every bundled data source (market history and tax tables) was audited for its terms and recorded, with the
  attribution each requires, in a data-licence file.
- An openly licensed UK market history was built to replace the current UK series, whose terms do not allow
  commercial use. It is not yet switched on, so UK plans still use the existing series, which ends in 2020.
