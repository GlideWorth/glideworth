title: The hosted service is open
date: 2026-11-16
tags: [api, mcp, security, web]

- **Glideworth Cloud is open for sign-ups.** It runs the same open-source software for you: a 30-day free trial
  with no card, then $9 a month or $90 a year.
- **Accounts, billing and email:** sign-up with email and password, subscriptions through Stripe, and
  email for sign-up confirmation, security notices and billing notices.
- **Two-factor sign-in** with an authenticator app and recovery codes.
- **Automatic encrypted backups,** one key per workspace, and an export of a whole workspace.
- **A hosted API and MCP endpoint** at a stable HTTPS address with OAuth, so AI assistants connect without a tunnel.
- **Email check-in summaries** for scheduled check-ins, which also still work through webhooks.
- The source repository is public: https://github.com/GlideWorth/glideworth.
