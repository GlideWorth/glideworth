Germany is estimated at a **basic level**: the main rules are in, with {facts.simplificationCount('de')} written simplifications,
but it has had less checking than the United Kingdom or the United States. The tax tables are for 2026
(Veranlagungszeitraum 2026), checked against the statutes and the federal ministries' published values on
19 November 2026. Later years index them with the plan's threshold policy.

The Einkommensteuerreformgesetz 2027 (a basic allowance of €12,564 in 2027 and €12,900 in 2028, a €1,430 employee
allowance and a flatter tariff) was a cabinet draft on that date and is **not applied**.

## What is modelled

- **Income tax** uses the §32a tariff for 2026: a basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag) of €12,348, then a rising rate
  to the top of the second zone at €17,799, a third zone to €69,878, a 42% zone to €277,825 and 45% above. Married
  couples are assessed jointly with splitting.
- **Solidarity surcharge**: 5.5% of income tax, with an exemption limit of €20,350 (€40,700 jointly) and a transition
  zone at 11.9%. Church tax is a setting: 9%, or 8% in Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg.
- **Employee social contributions**: the pension scheme at 18.6% in total (the employee pays half), unemployment at
  2.6% (half), statutory health at 14.6% plus the average supplementary rate of 2.9% (each shared between employer and
  employee), and long-term care at 3.6%. Ceilings are €101,400 for pension and €69,750 for health and care.
- **Long-term care surcharge**: people aged 23 or over with no children pay 0.6 points more. The model applies it when
  the household has no dependants under 25, unless you set "Has children, including grown-up ones" (parents never pay
  it). People born before 1940 are exempt. Each child under 25 from the second to the fifth takes 0.25 points off.
- **Capital income**: Abgeltungsteuer of 25% with a saver's allowance of €1,000, and a 30% partial exemption (Teilfreistellung)
  for equity funds, which Glideworth assumes by default.
- **Pensions**: the taxable share of a statutory pension is 84% for pensions starting in 2026 (82.5% for 2023, rising
  by half a point a year to 100% in 2058, under the Wachstumschancengesetz). The share applies to the whole pension
  each year. Rürup, Riester and bAV accounts are available. bAV contributions are tax-free up to 8% of the pension
  ceiling and social-contribution-free up to 4%. Riester contributions are deducted up to €2,100. Payouts from these
  contracts are available from age 62.
- **Aktivrente**: from 2026, wages for work done from the month after the statutory pension age are tax-free up to
  €24,000 a year (§3 Nr. 21 EStG). Without a birth month, the birthday is taken as 1 July.
- **Statutory pension age** follows the birth year: 65 for people born up to 1946, rising to 67 for those born from
  1964, and deferral adds 0.5% a month. You enter the pension amount yourself.

## Worked example

  engine does not round to whole euros as the law does. It is not a forecast or advice.
</Callout>

A single person has taxable income (zu versteuerndes Einkommen) of €40,000 in 2026. That falls in the third zone
(€17,799 to €69,878), where tax = (173.10 × z + 2,397) × z + 1,034.87 and z = (€40,000 − €17,799) ÷ 10,000.

- z = 22,201 ÷ 10,000 = 2.2201.
- 173.10 × 2.2201 = 384.30, plus 2,397 = 2,781.30; times 2.2201 = 6,174.76.
- Income tax = 6,174.76 + 1,034.87 = **€7,209.63**.
- The solidarity surcharge is nil, because the tax is below the €20,350 exemption limit.

Glideworth's tests compare salaries from €20,000 to €300,000 with the Federal Ministry of Finance's own wage-tax
calculator for 2026. The estimate is €2 to €17 higher in each case. That is because the official wage-tax calculation
deducts slightly more health contribution than the annual assessment does, and rounds down to whole euros.

## Historical data for simulations

No German or euro-area market history is bundled. German households are tested against US history by default
(S&P 500, 10-year Treasuries, T-bills and CPI-U, 1928 to 2025, in US dollars), so returns and inflation are
American.

## What to treat with care

- Tax is not rounded to whole euros.
- The 2027 reform is not applied; later years are the 2026 tables indexed with inflation.
- The Besteuerungsanteil is applied to the whole pension each year, and the fixed euro allowance is not frozen.
- Dividends and gains are assumed to come from equity funds. Set the equity-funds option off if they do not.
- Riester, bAV and Rürup payouts are fully taxable except Rürup, which uses the taxable share. Riester is replaced for
  new contracts from 2027 (Altersvorsorgereformgesetz); the deduction limit for the new products is not confirmed, so
  €2,100 is used for every year.
- Employees past the statutory pension age still pay the employee pension and unemployment contributions in the model,
  though in law they are usually exempt.
- Company pensions pay full health contributions with no monthly allowance.
- A figure for the self-employed leaves out their social contributions.
- No preset has been checked line by line against an official calculator for every case.
