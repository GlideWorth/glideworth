A plan is a forecast. **Progress** records what actually happens, so you can compare the two.

## Update balances and record a snapshot

<Steps>
1. Open **Progress** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Update balances**. Enter new balances for accounts, real-asset values (and loan balances) and debts.
   The update is all-or-nothing. A note is optional.
3. Save. A progress snapshot records your net worth for that date.
</Steps>

At most one automatic snapshot is kept per day: a second update the same day updates the day's snapshot instead
of adding another. A snapshot you record yourself, or one from an import, is left alone. A setting turns automatic
snapshots off. You can also backfill history with a bulk import of a net-worth series.

The page can plot net worth, assets and debts, or per-account history, over a range, and overlay a plan to compare
actual with planned.

## Check-ins

A check-in re-runs your plan (projection, chance of success, assumption audit) and stores the result in a plan
health history. By default the server runs one for each household's main plan weekly, with the first run as the
baseline. The cadence can be daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly. Set it under **Settings**, then **Check-ins**.

A check-in raises a notification for a material change:

- the chance of success moved by more than a threshold (default 5 percentage points);
- a milestone slipped later (default 6 months) or is no longer reached;
- a new warning or critical audit finding appeared;
- your net worth is more than a threshold (default 10 percent) below what the last check-in projected.

Notifications appear in the app's notification centre, and the same events go to webhooks.

## What this does not do

- The self-hosted app does not send email. Check-ins work through the app and webhooks; the hosted service also emails a summary.
- It does not read your accounts. You enter balances.
- The "behind plan" test is an approximation in today's money, interpolated across the year.
