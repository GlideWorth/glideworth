This policy is part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms/) and applies from 16 November 2026, when the hosted service opened. It covers the hosted service at
app.glideworth.com, including the REST API and the MCP server. It does not limit what the open-source licence
lets you do with the code on your own machine.

## 1. Do not

**Harm the service or other customers**

- Try to get into another customer's workspace or data, or look for weaknesses on live customer data. If you
  find a vulnerability, test only with accounts you own and report it under the [security
  policy](/security/).
- Overload the service, or work around rate limits, quotas, trial limits or entitlements. Do not create more than
  one trial for yourself, or use disposable email addresses to get around that.
- Run automated sign-ups, password guessing, scanning or load tests against the hosted service.
- Introduce malware, or send requests meant to break or slow the service.

**Misuse accounts and credentials**

- Share a login between people, or sell, rent or resell access.
- Use someone else's account or credentials, or keep a key or connection that its owner has revoked.
- Give an AI assistant or script a key with wider scopes than it needs, or leave a key where others can read it.
  You are responsible for what is done with your keys.

**Misuse the content**

- Enter data you have no right to enter, or personal data about other people without a lawful reason. Advisers
  holding client data should use the [data processing agreement](/legal/dpa/).
- Store anything unlawful, or anything unrelated to financial planning in notes or file fields, such as illegal
  material or large files that are not planning data.
- Use the service to give regulated financial advice to others, or to present Glideworth's estimates as
  personal advice to a client, without making clear what they are and without any authorisation you need. The
  service is not an advice tool.
- Use the service for fraud, money laundering, market abuse or evading tax or sanctions.

**Misuse the API and MCP server**

- Build a service that resells our API or results to third parties, or that stores results in a way that
  replaces the service, without our written agreement.
- Scrape public share links in bulk.
- Point webhooks at addresses you do not control, or at internal networks (these are blocked).
- Use prompts, tools or other techniques to make a connected AI assistant act outside the scopes of its key.

**Misuse the brand**

- Pretend to be Glideworth, or use the name or logo in a way that suggests we run or endorse your product. See the
  <RepoLink path="TRADEMARKS.md">trademark policy</RepoLink>.

## 2. Fair use

The API and MCP server are included in the plan within the limits in the terms (section 9). Normal use by one
household, its AI assistants and its own scripts is what the limits are for. If you need more, ask us at
<a href={`mailto:${site.contact.support}`}>{site.contact.support}</a>.

## 3. What we may do

If we think a rule has been broken we may, in proportion to the problem: warn you; slow or block requests; revoke
keys, share links or webhooks; make a workspace read-only; suspend an account; or end the contract as set out in
the terms. We will tell you why unless the law or security stops us. Even when an account is suspended, the
owner can export and delete their data. We may report unlawful conduct to the authorities.

## 4. Reporting a problem

To report abuse of the service or a copy of Glideworth that pretends to be us, write to
<a href={`mailto:${site.contact.security}`}>{site.contact.security}</a> for security matters, or
<a href={`mailto:${site.contact.support}`}>{site.contact.support}</a> for anything else.
